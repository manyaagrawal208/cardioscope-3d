How it works and limitations

Multimodal inputs: demographics and vitals, lipid panel and inflammation marker, exercise ECG findings (ST depression, peak heart rate), symptoms, and coronary calcium score from CT imaging.

Model: a transparent additive logistic model. Coefficients are set from the direction and rough size of well-known risk-factor effects (age, sex, blood pressure, LDL, HDL, smoking, diabetes, calcium score, symptoms and stress-test findings). A second logistic equation gives the 10-year chance of a major cardiac event. Every term is shown as a driver so the result can be explained.

3D mapping: overall risk is shifted per artery (left anterior descending highest, left main lowest) and per segment (proximal highest, distal lowest). Segment colour, vessel narrowing and plaque deposits all follow that score.

Limitations: this is a research prototype, not a medical device. The coefficients are illustrative and not trained or validated on a clinical cohort, and per-segment narrowing is a population-level estimate, not an angiogram. Replace lg() and r10() with a trained model (for example gradient boosting on Cleveland or Framingham data) without changing the 3D layer. Always confirm with a clinician.
